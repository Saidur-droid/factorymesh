## What gets FactoryMesh above 90

The fastest path is not more features. It is verified commercial proof:

- 5–10 real export-ready factories;
- fresh capacity snapshots with provenance;
- 2–3 real buyer briefs;
- 3 real capacity reservations;
- at least 1 order reaching shipment;
- first collected FactoryMesh fee;
- at least one repeat buyer signal;
- measured capacity accuracy;
- measured time-to-credible-match;
- first real per-order contribution margin;
- evidence that operator effort falls as the workflow repeats.

## Investor attack questions

### Market and urgency

**Why is this a venture-scale problem?**  
Answer with buyer economics, export scale and repeat manufacturing volume. Do not use market size alone as proof of product demand.

**Why now?**  
Show why buyers/factories are ready to expose and consume structured capacity data now. This remains partly a validation question until interviews and pilots prove it.

**Why Bangladesh first?**  
Dense export manufacturing, founder access and a narrow category create a practical starting network. Bangladesh must be a wedge, not a ceiling.

### Competition

**Why not Merchant Bay?**  
Do not attack them. Demonstrate that FactoryMesh returns verified, time-bound, reservable capacity and route-level execution evidence for a buyer brief.

**Why not NXRG?**  
Do not claim they are "only a directory." Their public platform includes verification and structured RFQ. Prove that slot-level freshness, confidence and reservation produce a better outcome.

**Why not FOURSOURCE or Sewport?**  
Their scale validates digital sourcing. FactoryMesh must win a narrower job: capacity availability and executable routing, then expand only after proof.

**What if a competitor copies your UI?**  
UI is not the moat. The intended moat is accumulated capacity-declaration accuracy + reservation history + production/QC/shipment outcomes.

### Supply-side truth

**Why will factories reveal real capacity?**  
Hypothesis: qualified demand and better utilization. Evidence needed: onboarding conversion, update compliance and real slot confirmations.

**What stops factories exaggerating availability?**  
Freshness decay, source provenance, confirmation, reservation outcomes and declaration-accuracy history.

**How frequently must capacity update?**  
Pilot rule: weekly baseline refresh and tighter confirmation for active matches. The correct cadence must be validated category by category.

### Buyer behavior

**Why will buyers trust an algorithm?**  
Do not require blind trust. Use deterministic constraints, explainable scoring and human review until outcome data supports more automation.

**Why won't buyers keep using existing sourcing relationships?**  
Target overflow, urgent, backup and Bangladesh-entry demand where relationship networks are weakest or slowest.

**Why won't buyer and factory bypass you?**  
If FactoryMesh only introduces them, they probably will. Ongoing planning, reservation, production visibility, exception handling and outcome memory must create continuing value.

### Economics

**Why 2.5%?**  
It is a validation price, not a proven market price. The first five paid shipped orders must test willingness to pay and cost-to-serve.

**Are you software or a sourcing agency?**  
This is a core diligence risk. Track operator hours per order and per $100k GMV. Software leverage must improve with repetition.

**Who carries working-capital or inventory risk?**  
FactoryMesh is asset-light first and should not fund production from its own balance sheet in the early model.

### Trust and liability

**Who is responsible if quality fails?**  
Commercial terms, factory responsibility, QC evidence and partner workflows must define this before real transactions scale.

**How do you verify compliance?**  
Store source/version/evidence and never turn stale certification metadata into a guarantee.

**What happens when capacity becomes stale?**  
Confidence decays; low-confidence slots must not be presented as executable without re-verification.

### Moat

**What proprietary asset exists after 100 orders?**  
A graph of real capacity accuracy, quotes, reservations, delays, defects, cancellations, cost variance and buyer routing behavior.

**Why can a brand not build this internally?**  
A brand can build planning software for its own network. FactoryMesh must become valuable because it sees multi-buyer/multi-factory patterns and offers network access.

### Fundraising proof

**What would make this investable rather than interesting?**  
Paid, repeatable execution evidence.

Minimum evidence package:
1. real factory agreements/participation;
2. real capacity snapshots;
3. real buyer brief;
4. route recommendation and confirmation history;
5. reservation record;
6. production/QC/shipment evidence;
7. invoice and payment proof;
8. repeat buyer signal;
9. unit economics;
10. data-quality methodology.

## Fundraising rule

Do not raise on:

> "We built an AI marketplace for a $38B industry."

Raise on:

> "We proved that a buyer can send one production brief, receive verified bookable capacity faster, reserve it, execute the order, pay us, and come back — while each transaction improves our capacity and reliability dataset."
