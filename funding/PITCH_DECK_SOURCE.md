# FactoryMesh — Canonical Pitch Deck Source

This is the source text for the fundraising deck. Do not add unverified traction.

## Slide 1 — Company

# FactoryMesh
### Programmable manufacturing capacity for global brands

**Bangladesh RMG first. Global manufacturing control plane later.**

One-line promise:

> For your exact order, show which factory is truly available now, how much capacity is available, how fast it can produce, expected cost and execution risk — then reserve that capacity.

---

## Slide 2 — The problem

Global buyers do not primarily lack factory lists.

They lack **trusted, time-bound, executable capacity**.

Today the workflow is fragmented across:
- existing relationships
- buying houses
- emails/calls/WhatsApp
- spreadsheets
- RFQs
- static supplier profiles
- manual capacity confirmation

The operational question remains hard:

> **Where can this exact order be produced, during this exact time window, at acceptable cost and risk?**

---

## Slide 3 — The solution

FactoryMesh turns fragmented capacity into a **verified, bookable and routable production network**.

**Order Brief → Verified Capacity → Intelligent Routing → Reservation → Production → QC → Shipment → Outcome History**

Buyer gets:
- 1–3 executable capacity-backed options
- expected cost and lead time
- compliance/quality/risk reasoning
- recommended one-factory or split route
- reservation
- execution visibility
- backup route when needed

---

## Slide 4 — The product edge

Five primitives:

1. **Live 12-week Capacity Map**
2. **Capacity Confidence Score**
3. **Order Routing Engine**
4. **Capacity Reservation**
5. **Real Outcome History**

Competitor-style answer:

> “These factories can make hoodies.”

FactoryMesh target answer:

> “A is booked. B has 65k units but weaker delivery reliability. C has 100k available in your window and meets the required compliance. Route 70k to C + 30k to D. Reserve now.”

---

## Slide 5 — Why now / why Bangladesh

Bangladesh already has deep apparel manufacturing density.

The opportunity is not to create manufacturing supply.

It is to make existing supply:
- visible
- time-bound
- comparable
- reservable
- routable
- measurable

FactoryMesh starts with a narrow Bangladesh RMG wedge and expands only after network density and outcome data are proven.

For external market statistics, use current BGMEA/EPB/official export data in the final deck and record source/date on the slide.

---

## Slide 6 — Business model

Launch pricing:

**2.5% of fulfilled/shipped FOB manufacturing GMV**

- $1,000 minimum successful-order fee
- 2.0% possible for $250k+ orders where economics justify it
- negotiated volume pricing for large programs
- no mandatory buyer subscription at launch
- factory onboarding free initially
- no successful order = no standard orchestration fee

Illustrative economics:
- $100k order → $2,500 revenue
- 10 repeat buyers × $100k fulfilled GMV/month × 2.5% → $25k monthly revenue

These are scenarios, not current traction.
