## Core ONKUR narrative

> Bangladesh is one of the world's most important apparel manufacturing hubs, but usable production capacity is still coordinated through fragmented relationships, spreadsheets, calls and stale supplier information. FactoryMesh turns factory capacity into verified, bookable and routable infrastructure. A buyer submits one production brief; FactoryMesh identifies who can actually produce it now, how much capacity is available, expected cost/lead time/risk, recommends the safest route, reserves the slot and tracks execution through shipment. Every completed order improves a proprietary capacity-and-outcome graph.
