# FactoryMesh — USD 1.0M Seed Round Plan

## Round target

**USD 1,000,000**

This is a fundraising target, not committed capital.

### Preferred structure

- **~USD 500k:** credible regional/global institutional lead
- **~USD 500k:** target ONKUR/BSIC participation

Reason:
BSIC publicly describes a co-investment process in which the founder first secures a credible institutional lead and BSIC then invests matching capital alongside that lead.

The exact BSIC cheque, matching mechanics, instrument and final allocation must be negotiated. Do not present this target structure as an approved BSIC offer.

## Why USD 1M

FactoryMesh is not raising primarily to build an app.

The core MVP already exists.

Capital is intended to convert software into a trusted two-sided manufacturing network and proprietary outcome dataset.

The round must buy:
- production launch
- factory density
- international buyer acquisition
- verification operations
- QC/trust infrastructure
- transaction history
- data moat
- institutional governance

## Hiring logic

Do not build a large team before transactions exist.

### Initial hires / contracts
1. strong Bangladesh RMG commercial/operations lead
2. factory onboarding/verification operator
3. buyer sales / business development
4. product/engineering support only where founder/AI-assisted build capacity is insufficient
5. fractional finance/legal/compliance
6. QC/inspection through partners first where practical

### Avoid early
- large executive team
- expensive office
- broad marketing team
- country expansion teams
- own QC workforce at scale
- own logistics assets
- lending balance sheet

## Capital milestones

### Milestone 1 — Proof
- production live
- 10+ verified factories
- 3+ buyer briefs
- 3 reservations
- 1 shipment
- first paid fee

### Milestone 2 — Repeatability
- 20+ verified factories
- 5 active/repeat buyers
- $500k+ cumulative fulfilled/reserved GMV target
- measured capacity accuracy
- measured contribution margin
- declining operator effort per order

### Milestone 3 — Network density
- 50 verified factories
- 10 repeat buyers
- $1M monthly fulfilled GMV run-rate target
- $25k monthly revenue target at 2.5%
- capacity-confidence model supported by real data

### Milestone 4 — Seed-to-A bridge
- 100 verified factories
- 20 active/repeat buyers
- $3M monthly fulfilled GMV run-rate target
- ~$60k monthly revenue at ~2% blended take
- positive contribution margin
- >85% capacity accuracy target
- evidence that network density improves conversion/routing speed

## Revenue model

Launch:
**fulfilled manufacturing GMV × orchestration take rate**

Base:
**2.5%**

Example target economics:
- $1M monthly fulfilled GMV → $25k revenue/month
- $3M monthly fulfilled GMV at 2% blended rate → $60k revenue/month

Do not confuse GMV with revenue.

## Runway discipline

The goal of the Seed round is not maximum burn.

The goal is to reach the next financing stage with:
- repeat buyer demand
- transaction history
- positive/improving contribution margin
- proprietary data
- credible global expansion logic

If buyer demand or capacity accuracy fails, reduce burn and redesign before scaling.
