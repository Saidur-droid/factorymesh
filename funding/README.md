# FactoryMesh Fundraising Control Center

**Status date:** 2026-09-20  
**Primary target:** ONKUR — Bangladesh Fund I / BSIC  
**Round strategy:** Target a **USD 1.0M Seed round**, ideally with a credible regional/global institutional lead and ONKUR/BSIC participating alongside it. This is a financing target, not a claim that capital is committed.

## Rule

Do not submit a weak application just because the fund is open.

FactoryMesh should approach institutional investors with evidence that the company has moved beyond an idea:

**working product → production-live → verified factories → real buyer briefs → reservations → shipped orders → paid orchestration revenue → repeat demand**

No document in this folder authorizes anyone to invent traction, customers, revenue, partnerships, investor interest, factory commitments, or government/BSIC endorsement.

## Why FactoryMesh fits ONKUR

BSIC publicly positions ONKUR as institutional venture capital for high-growth Bangladeshi startups. Its public materials explicitly model opportunity across **SaaS / AI** and **RMG infrastructure**, and its co-investment model is designed to invest alongside credible regional/global institutional VC leads.

FactoryMesh sits at the intersection:

**Bangladesh RMG infrastructure × SaaS/data network × AI-assisted manufacturing orchestration**

Canonical company promise:

> **For your exact order, show which factory is truly available now, how much capacity is available, how fast it can produce, the expected cost and execution risk — then let you reserve that capacity immediately.**

## Fundraise readiness gate

### Gate A — company readiness
- legal operating entity confirmed
- founder/shareholder cap table documented
- founder IP assigned to the company
- bank account and accounting records ready
- tax/statutory compliance reviewed
- material contracts stored
- privacy/terms/commercial responsibilities reviewed

### Gate B — product readiness
- production deployment live
- real Auth/DB/Storage working
- buyer + factory live E2E complete
- cross-tenant isolation verified
- concurrent capacity reservation protection verified
- monitoring/backups/recovery baseline working

### Gate C — market proof
Minimum target before calling the round institutional-ready:
- 10+ verified factories in the first narrow category
- 3+ genuine buyer briefs
- 3+ real capacity reservations
- 1+ shipped order
- first paid FactoryMesh orchestration fee
- evidence that at least one buyer wants to repeat
- initial capacity-accuracy evidence
- measured operator effort per order

### Gate D — co-investor proof
- investor one-pager final
- pitch deck final
- data room complete
- target lead-investor list
- warm introductions pursued
- at least one credible institutional investor in active diligence or serious round discussion
