# FactoryMesh — Investor One-Pager

## Programmable manufacturing capacity for global brands

**Bangladesh RMG first. Global manufacturing control plane later.**

### Problem

Global apparel buyers can discover factories, but they still struggle to answer the operational question that matters:

> **Who can actually produce this exact order, in this exact time window, at acceptable cost and execution risk?**

Capacity is fragmented, stale, relationship-driven and difficult to compare. Buyers lose time across emails, calls, spreadsheets, sourcing intermediaries and manual follow-up.

### Solution

FactoryMesh converts fragmented factory capacity into **verified, bookable and routable production infrastructure**.

A buyer submits an order brief or tech pack. FactoryMesh returns:
- verified available capacity
- realistic cost/lead-time range
- 1–3 executable options
- capability/compliance/risk reasoning
- one-factory or split-order routing
- reservation path
- production/QC/shipment visibility
- backup routing when needed

### Buyer promise

> **For your exact order, show which factory is truly available now, how much capacity is available, how fast it can produce, the expected cost and execution risk — then let you reserve that capacity immediately.**

### Initial wedge

**Bangladesh RMG — starting narrow with knit tops / hoodies / t-shirts and export-ready factories.**

The wedge is not “more supplier profiles.”

It is:

**Live Capacity → Confidence → Routing → Reservation → Outcome History**

### Business model

Launch:
- **2.5% of fulfilled/shipped FOB manufacturing GMV**
- $1,000 minimum successful-order fee
- large-order volume pricing where justified
- no mandatory subscription initially
- factory onboarding free initially

Later:
- buyer planning SaaS
- factory premium tools
- enterprise API
- QC/logistics/insurance/finance partner economics

### Data moat

> **Factory → Capability → Capacity Window → Buyer → Quote → Reservation → Production → QC → Shipment → Actual Outcome**

Each real order improves:
- capacity-declaration accuracy
- factory reliability
- cost behavior
- routing quality
- reservation conversion
- exception intelligence
- buyer demand patterns

### Product status

Core MVP codebase and interactive E2E are built.

The product includes buyer/factory/operator workflows, capacity modeling, deterministic routing, reservation logic, production milestones, QC/shipment flow, Supabase schema/RLS, tests and browser E2E.

**Production-live and market traction are the immediate gates.**

### Why Bangladesh can win

FactoryMesh is not trying to replace factories, ERP systems or sourcing relationships.

It is building the routing layer above them — starting where manufacturing density is already globally significant and operational coordination remains fragmented.
