# FactoryMesh Co-Investor Strategy

## Why a lead investor matters

BSIC publicly describes a co-investment flow in which a founder secures a credible regional/global institutional lead and BSIC then participates alongside that lead.

Therefore FactoryMesh should run two fundraising processes in parallel:

1. **ONKUR / BSIC relationship**
2. **credible institutional lead investor search**

Do not wait for one before starting the other.

## Target lead-investor profile

Prioritize investors with at least one of:

- emerging-market B2B software
- supply-chain / logistics technology
- marketplaces with strong transaction economics
- manufacturing technology
- Bangladesh / South Asia mandate
- export/international commerce
- Seed-stage cheque capacity around $250k–$750k+
- willingness to lead or anchor a round

## High-signal ecosystem names

BSIC's May 2026 launch included representatives from several international investment organizations, publicly reported to include:
- VentureSouq
- Wavemaker Partners
- 500 Global
- Plug and Play
- ADB Ventures
- GFR Fund
- Sturgeon Capital
- Conjunction Capital
- Orbit Startups

These names are **relationship/research targets, not endorsements and not claims of FactoryMesh fit or investor interest**.

Before outreach, research each fund's:
- current fund status
- geography
- stage
- cheque size
- sector thesis
- recent investments
- lead-investor behavior
- partner most relevant to FactoryMesh

## Investor message

Do not lead with:

> “We want ONKUR money.”

Lead with:

> **“We are building the live capacity-routing layer for Bangladesh RMG. The product is built; we are converting it into real factory density, routed GMV and a proprietary execution dataset. We are opening a USD 1M Seed round and looking for an institutional lead that understands B2B infrastructure / supply chain / emerging markets.”**

Then explain that BSIC/ONKUR is a potential domestic institutional co-investor.

## Lead-investor evidence package

Send in this order:
1. one-line intro
2. one-pager
3. product demo
4. traction dashboard
5. deck
6. data room after investor qualifies

Do not send a giant data room cold.

## Outreach pipeline

Maintain:
- investor
- partner
- geography
- thesis fit
- cheque range
- warm intro source
- date contacted
- response
- next step
- objections
- diligence status
- lead probability (internal only)
- notes

## Best timing

Start relationship building immediately.

Ask for a lead commitment only when FactoryMesh can show:
- production-live product
- real factories
- real buyers
- real reservations
- first shipped GMV / revenue
- clear next 18-month milestones

A credible lead plus real commercial proof materially strengthens the ONKUR case.
