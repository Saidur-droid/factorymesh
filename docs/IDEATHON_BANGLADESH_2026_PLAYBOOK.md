## FactoryMesh competition thesis

### One sentence

**FactoryMesh turns fragmented Bangladesh garment production capacity into verified, time-bound, routable and reservable infrastructure for global apparel buyers.**

### The problem

A buyer may know many factories exist and still not know:
- which one can make this exact product;
- which one has usable capacity in the required window;
- what quantity is really available;
- what lead time/cost/risk is realistic;
- whether one or multiple factories should be used;
- whether the proposed slot can actually be committed.

The failure is not "there are no factory directories."

The failure is:

> **discovery does not equal executable capacity.**

### The solution

A buyer submits an order brief / tech pack.

FactoryMesh:
1. normalizes hard requirements;
2. filters impossible options;
3. checks time-bound capacity;
4. applies capacity confidence;
5. ranks one-factory or split routes;
6. explains warnings/trade-offs;
7. supports capacity reservation;
8. follows production/QC/shipment;
9. records outcomes to improve future routing.

### The memorable demo

Buyer enters:

> 100,000 hoodies, target price, compliance requirements and ship-by date.

The judge sees:
- Factory A rejected because the required window is unavailable;
- Factory B partially fits but has weaker confidence/reliability;
- Factory C + D create the best executable route;
- the exact production slots and quantity are visible;
- buyer reserves the route;
- production/QC/shipment timeline becomes auditable;
- outcome data updates future confidence.

## Why this is not another marketplace

Never say:
> "There are no competitors."

Say:
> "Digital sourcing exists. FactoryMesh focuses on a narrower operational decision: which capacity is actually usable for this exact order now, how confident are we, and can we reserve it?"

Competitors/substitutes include:
- Merchant Bay;
- NXRG;
- FOURSOURCE;
- Sewport;
- traditional buying houses;
- brand internal sourcing teams.

The competition advantage must be demonstrated through **better execution evidence**, not adjectives.

## Round 1 answer architecture

### 1. Problem
Explain one operational bottleneck with a concrete buyer scenario.

### 2. Why Bangladesh
Use current official/BGMEA facts, but do not imply the whole export market is FactoryMesh's obtainable market.

### 3. Solution
Explain live capacity, routing, confidence and reservation in plain language.

### 4. What is already built
Use only repository-verified facts:
- working MVP codebase;
- buyer/factory/operator workflows;
- deterministic routing;
- reservation flow;
- production/QC/shipment workflow;
- automated tests/browser E2E.

Do **not** claim live commercial traction without external evidence.

### 5. Business model
State that the current launch model is a success/orchestration fee on fulfilled GMV, with the first paid orders explicitly serving as pricing validation.

### 6. Scalability
Bangladesh first; narrow category density first; then adjacent categories and other manufacturing countries only after the core loop works.

### 7. Why the team can execute
Use actual founder/team facts only. Do not invent industry partnerships, advisors or customer relationships.

## Proof targets before a later-round live pitch

Targets, not current claims:
- production deployment passes real-user smoke tests;
- 5–10 real export-ready factories are verified;
- at least 2 real buyer briefs;
- at least 1 real capacity reservation;
- measured time-to-credible-match;
- capacity freshness/accuracy sample;
- real demo using clearly labeled real vs synthetic/demo data;
- one buyer interview quote or documented pain statement with consent.

If shipment/payment proof exists by the pitch, use it. If not, do not fabricate it.

## Judge/investor questions to rehearse

1. Why is this not a buying house?
2. Why will factories share capacity?
3. What stops false capacity declarations?
4. Why would a buyer pay 2.5%?
5. Why not Merchant Bay / NXRG / FOURSOURCE?
6. What happens if the buyer and factory bypass you?
7. How do you handle quality disputes?
8. What is AI doing today versus later?
9. What data becomes proprietary?
10. Why Bangladesh first?
11. How do you expand globally without becoming operations-heavy?
12. What proof exists today?
13. What would the investment fund?
14. Which metric would make you stop or redesign?

## Competition truth boundary

Never present:
- target factory count as current factory count;
- simulated capacity as live capacity;
- test reservation as a commercial reservation;
- demo GMV as real GMV;
- planned 2.5% pricing as validated willingness to pay;
- investor conversations as funding;
- interested contacts as signed customers/partners.

The strongest pitch is evidence-backed and explicit about what is built, what is measured and what remains to be proven.

## Competition operating principle

**Do not build to look like a winner. Build the smallest real proof that makes the business more investable even if the competition did not exist.**
